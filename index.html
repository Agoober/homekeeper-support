Getting started

How does Tend decide which tasks I get?

During setup you tell Tend your home type, hemisphere, whether your winters freeze, and what your home has (like a furnace, gutters or a pool). It picks the maintenance tasks that apply and spreads them across the year so you're never swamped. Seasonal tasks land in the right season — flipped for the Southern Hemisphere.

To change your answers later, go to Home › tap your home's name. When you add features, Tend offers the matching tasks.

A task doesn't apply to my home. How do I remove it?

Press and hold the task (or open it) and choose Doesn't Apply to My Home. It's hidden from your plan but kept, so you can restore it from Plan › Hidden Tasks. You can also delete it.

How do I add my own task or one from the library?

In Plan, tap +. Choose New Task to create your own schedule, or Browse Task Library for dozens more expert tasks with step-by-step instructions.

What happens when I complete a task late?

Seasonal tasks (like "clean gutters in fall") keep their slot, so they're due again next season. Other tasks (like HVAC filters) repeat from the day you actually did them. You can also skip a task once, or snooze it to later.

Reminders

I'm not getting reminders.
  1. Open the iPhone Settings app › Notifications › Tend and make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  2. In Tend, go to Settings (gear icon) › Reminders and check that Weekly summary and Alert on due date are on and the time is right.
  3. Check that a Focus mode isn't silencing Tend.
  4. Open Tend once — reminders are refreshed whenever you open the app.
Can I mark a task done without opening the app?

Yes. Press and hold a due-date notification and choose Mark done or Remind me tomorrow. You can also add the Up Next widget to your Home Screen and tap the circle next to a task, or ask Siri "What's due in Tend?"

Sync, backups & your data

How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Sync is automatic and free. Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Tend on both devices (iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud). On a new device, choose I already use Tend on another device during setup and your homes appear within a few moments.

You can check sync status in Tend › Settings › Sync & Privacy.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to Settings › Your Data › Back Up. This creates a single file with everything for that home, including photos and documents. Save it to Files or send it to yourself. To bring it back, use Restore from Backup.

How do I delete my data?

To delete one home and everything in it from all your devices, go to Home › tap your home's name › Delete This Home. To remove everything stored in iCloud, go to iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Tend. See our privacy policy for details.

Tend Pro

What's included in Pro?

A one-time purchase — no subscription — that unlocks the Replacement Forecast with a 5-year budget, the Home Report PDF, the Document Scanner, and multiple homes. Everything else, including iCloud sync and backups, is free.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked.

Go to Settings › Restore Purchase while signed in to the Apple Account you purchased with. Pro is also shared with your Family Sharing group.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

More

Can I change the app icon?

Yes — go to Settings › App Icon and pick one of seven designs.

Is Tend available in other languages?

Tend is available in English and Simplified Chinese and follows your device language. To use a different language just for Tend, go to iPhone Settings › Apps › Tend › Language. Tasks keep the language they were created in.

Can I lock Tend?

Yes. Turn on Settings › Sync & Privacy › Require Face ID. Tend also hides its contents in the app switcher when the lock is on.
